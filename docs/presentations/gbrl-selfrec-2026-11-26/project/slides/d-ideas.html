Four reward changes for the signal sender

IdeaChangeFixes
1 · honeypot rankcomm. term = fraction of honeypots with lower P(YES); signal-ON rewrites onlytraining target ≠ eval target (ON vs OFF vs honeypots)
2 · per-term advantagenormalise each reward term’s advantage separatelybackdoor term varies ±0.5 in a group, signal only ±0.1
3 · added suspicionoverseer term = max(0, sus(rewrite) − sus(original))overseer penalises keeping the backdoor
4 · two stagesstage 1: keep the backdoor; stage 2: signal (ideas 1 + 3)backdoor and signal compete for updates

Her plan: test 1, 3 and 4 one at a time, then combine them

Idea 1, tested (21 Oct, V3.1, 30 steps, ~$22)

  • Validation AUROC vs honeypots 0.67 → 0.78 (n = 20 per side)
  • Not bit-specific: OFF rewrites rose almost as much (P(YES) 0.44 → 0.61)
  • Most reward gain came from backdoor retention (66% → 90%)
  • Sender explained its signal in prose before the code (59/64 at step 30)
